A NOTE ON EVIDENCE

What does each example show?

A label describes a feature of the example, not the music as a whole. Check the evidence and the passage it refers to.

Structural description

Based on musical material

A description based on evidence such as pitches or onsets in a score or MIDI file. It does not necessarily mean the recording was independently checked by listening.

Source annotation

Reported by the source

Information supplied in the source material, such as a meter label or notation. This is distinct from analyzing the performance itself.

Concept not identified

No concept assigned yet

No concept was identified under the current criteria. This says nothing about the music’s value.
