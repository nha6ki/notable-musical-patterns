03 / USE & ATTRIBUTION

Usage terms and verification

  • On-site audio consists of short excerpts with documented sources, terms, attribution and changes. Time signatures for Freesound recordings follow the uploader’s description and have not been independently verified. Performance recordings, electronic drum output and MIDI synthesis are distinguished.
  • External links open the provider’s page. This page does not load YouTube or Spotify players. Playback availability and a match to the exact recording or passage are not guaranteed.
  • Reference only entries include source references and external pages. Unverified audio and the original scores, MIDI files and databases are not bundled here. Coverage and usage terms
