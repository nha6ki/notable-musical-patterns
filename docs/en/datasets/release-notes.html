NOTABLE MUSICAL PATTERNS / COVERAGE

Coverage and usage terms

This catalogue contains 6,729 entries from 16 datasets and source collections: 2,983 have on-site audio and 3,746 do not. Of the latter, 578 link to listening pages at the source or to alternative performances. Players from external services are not embedded.

The playable catalogue includes 1,168 synthesized Mutopia excerpts. Counts by source describe this site’s coverage, not necessarily the size of the original dataset. An external provider is opened only when you follow its link. Availability may depend on region or account; an exact match to the recording or passage is not guaranteed.

CC BY 4.0 and CC BY 3.0 apply only to the materials they cover. They do not provide blanket permission for compositions, recordings or arrangements. See each result card for audio attribution and changes.
